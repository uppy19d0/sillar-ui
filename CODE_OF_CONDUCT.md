# Code of conduct

Sillar UI is an open, respectful community. We welcome people of every background, identity, experience level, and location.

Be considerate, give actionable feedback, respect different perspectives, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, and sharing private information without permission are not accepted.

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the contact method in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed to protect the community.
